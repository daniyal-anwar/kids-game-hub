PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

1. About Kids Game Hub

Kids Game Hub is a browser-based gaming website offering simple arcade and educational games. We aim to provide a fun and straightforward gaming experience.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The current version of Kids Game Hub does not require users to create an account. Some game and player information is stored locally in your web browser using browser storage technology called localStorage.

This may include:

  • Player name entered on the website
  • Game scores and best scores
  • XP and player level
  • Achievements
  • Games played

This information is currently stored on the user's device and is not stored in a Kids Game Hub database.

3. Personal Information

Kids Game Hub currently does not require users to provide their real name, address, telephone number, or other personal information in order to play the games.

Users should avoid entering sensitive or personally identifying information as their player name.

4. Children's Privacy

Kids Game Hub is designed with children and families in mind. We aim to minimize the collection of personal information and do not currently require children to create accounts to play the games.

We do not knowingly ask children to provide unnecessary personal information through the games.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The current version of Kids Game Hub primarily uses browser localStorage for game progress. If analytics, cookies, advertising services, or other third-party technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain their use.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of Kids Game Hub does not require users to connect third-party accounts to play the games.

Any future third-party services, such as analytics or advertising providers, will be reviewed and disclosed as appropriate.

7. Data Security

Because current game progress is stored locally in the user's browser, users can clear this information by clearing their browser's site data or local storage.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Kids Game Hub develops new features, including accounts, analytics, advertising, or other services.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Kids Game Hub, please contact us at hello@kidsgamehub.com .
